## IELTS: Environment phrases | B2 | Leaf | Key phrases for environment and climate topics.
carbon_footprint :: noun :: the total greenhouse gases that a person, product or activity produces :: Flying less is one way to cut your carbon footprint.
carbon_neutral :: adjective :: producing no more carbon than is removed or offset :: The company promises to be carbon neutral by 2035.
fossil_fuel :: noun :: coal, oil or gas formed from ancient plants and animals :: Burning fossil fuels is the main cause of global warming.
natural_disaster :: noun :: a sudden event in nature, such as a flood or earthquake, that causes great damage :: The country is recovering from a terrible natural disaster.
oil_spill :: noun :: oil that escapes into the sea or land and pollutes it :: The oil spill killed thousands of seabirds.
sea_level :: noun :: the average height of the sea's surface, used to measure other heights :: Rising sea level threatens many coastal cities.
noise_pollution :: noun :: loud or constant sound that harms people's health or comfort :: Noise pollution is a serious problem near airports.
light_pollution :: noun :: too much artificial light that hides the stars at night :: Light pollution makes it hard to see the stars in cities.
green_building :: noun :: a building designed to use less energy and water and to harm the environment less :: Green buildings can cut energy bills by half.
ground_water :: noun :: water found under the ground in soil and rocks :: Farms in the region depend on ground water.
heatwave :: noun :: a period of unusually hot weather :: The heatwave lasted for ten days.
overfishing :: noun :: catching so many fish that stocks cannot recover :: Overfishing has almost emptied some parts of the ocean.
natural_selection :: noun :: the process by which living things best suited to their environment survive and reproduce :: Darwin explained evolution through natural selection.
emissions :: noun :: gases or other substances sent into the air :: Many cities want to reduce traffic emissions.

child_labour :: noun :: the use of children to do regular work, often in bad conditions :: Child labour is still common in some countries.
asylum_seeker :: noun :: a person who asks another country to protect them from danger at home :: The asylum seeker waited months for a decision.
exchange_rate :: noun :: the value of one currency compared with another :: A weak exchange rate makes imports more expensive.
stock_market :: noun :: a place where shares in companies are bought and sold :: The stock market fell sharply on Monday.
credit_card :: noun :: a plastic card that lets you buy now and pay later :: Many students rely on a credit card to get through the month.
developing_country :: noun :: a poorer country that is building its industry and economy :: Clean water is still a problem in many developing countries.
living_standard :: noun :: the level of wealth and comfort a group of people have :: Living standards have risen steadily over the last fifty years.
poverty_line :: noun :: the income level below which a person is considered poor :: Millions of families live below the poverty line.
life_expectancy :: noun :: the average number of years a person is expected to live :: Life expectancy has risen thanks to better healthcare.
nuclear_family :: noun :: a family made up of two parents and their children :: The nuclear family is becoming less common in some countries.
generation_gap :: noun :: the difference in attitudes between older and younger people :: The generation gap can cause arguments at home.
peer_pressure :: noun :: the influence of people your own age to behave in a certain way :: Peer pressure often pushes teenagers to try smoking.
role_model :: noun :: a person whose behaviour others try to copy :: Athletes can be positive role models for children.
single_parent :: noun :: a mother or father who brings up children alone :: A single parent often has to balance work and childcare.
social_worker :: noun :: a professional who helps people with family or personal problems :: A social worker visited the family every week.
social_mobility :: noun :: the ability of people to move to a higher social or economic class :: Good schools improve social mobility.
welfare_state :: noun :: a system in which the government pays for health care, pensions and support for the poor :: The welfare state protects people who lose their jobs.
public_spending :: noun :: money that a government spends on services such as schools and hospitals :: Public spending on transport has increased this year.
prime_minister :: noun :: the head of government in many countries :: The prime minister announced new laws on Friday.
capital_punishment :: noun :: the legal killing of a person as punishment for a serious crime :: Many countries have abolished capital punishment.
life_sentence :: noun :: a punishment of imprisonment for the rest of a person's life :: He received a life sentence for murder.
community_service :: noun :: unpaid work for the public, given as a punishment instead of prison :: The teenagers were given community service for vandalism.
crime_rate :: noun :: the number of crimes committed in a place during a period of time :: The crime rate fell after more police were hired.
drug_abuse :: noun :: the harmful use of illegal drugs or medicines :: Drug abuse can destroy families.
domestic_violence :: noun :: violence between people who live in the same home :: Charities offer shelters to victims of domestic violence.

## IELTS: Linking phrases for essays | B2 | Link | Phrases that organise your writing.
as_a_result :: phrase :: because of what has just been mentioned :: He missed the train and, as a result, arrived late.
in_order_to :: phrase :: with the purpose of doing something :: She saved money in order to buy a laptop.
for_instance :: phrase :: used to give an example :: Some animals, for instance, sleep during the winter.
for_example :: phrase :: used to introduce an example :: Many countries, for example, offer free healthcare.
in_particular :: phrase :: especially or more than others :: Young people in particular use social media daily.
on_the_whole :: phrase :: in general, considering everything :: On the whole, the benefits outweigh the risks.
on_balance :: phrase :: after thinking carefully about all the points :: On balance, I believe public transport should be free.
by_and_large :: phrase :: in most cases; generally :: By and large, students enjoyed the course.
to_some_extent :: phrase :: partly but not completely :: To some extent, technology has made our lives easier.
in_conclusion :: phrase :: used to begin the last part of an essay :: In conclusion, governments must act now.
to_sum_up :: phrase :: used to introduce a short summary :: To sum up, there are strong arguments on both sides.
in_the_long_term :: phrase :: over a long period in the future :: In the long term, renewable energy will be cheaper.
in_the_short_term :: phrase :: over the next few days or weeks :: In the short term, prices are likely to rise.
due_to :: phrase :: because of :: The match was cancelled due to bad weather.
generally_speaking :: phrase :: in most cases :: Generally speaking, people live longer than before.
strictly_speaking :: phrase :: if you consider the exact meaning :: Strictly speaking, a tomato is a fruit.
